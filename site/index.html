An aluminium case for the ErgoDox EZ, written in Python.

A parametric two-piece case for CNC milling. It takes the stock EZ PCB, switches and keycaps, and adds tent legs that click into place on toothed bosses. One build123d script produces the STEP files for the shop, the STL files for a test print, and PDF drawings for the order.

What's in the box

Exact key layout

The switch positions, column stagger and the 25° thumb cluster come straight from the original ErgoDox KiCad PCB.

Integrated plate

A 1.5 mm switch plate is milled into the top shell and sits in a 4 mm key well. A separate 3 mm spacer with an opening per switch fills the gap down to the PCB.

Clamped PCB

The PCB sits between bosses in the top and posts in the bottom tray, held by 8 × M2.5 countersunk screws on the stock hole pattern.

Clicking tent legs

A tapped M3 hole with a Ø10 boss carrying 36 radial V-teeth. The custom leg's hub is its exact negative, held by the stock EZ spring thumbscrew.

Fits the real board

Pockets for the electrolytic capacitor on each PCB, measured from photos. Openings for TRRS, USB, LEDs and reset, as on the EZ.

Ready to order

Every release ships STEP files plus A3 PDF drawings with thread tables and tooth specs, and order remarks in English and Chinese.

From script to metal

  1. 01

    Print a test fit

    Grab the -stl.zip from the latest release and print the four case parts. Drop in your PCB and switches.

  2. 02

    Check your board

    Print ergodox_right_fitcheck.dxf at 100 % and work through the fit-check list.

  3. 03

    Order the parts

    Upload the -jlccnc-order.zip parts with their PDF drawings. The order guide has every form setting.

  4. 04

    Assemble

    16 × M2.5 countersunk screws, 8 rubber feet, 6 legs with TPU caps and the stock EZ thumbscrews. Bill of materials.

or build it yourself · Python 3.10–3.13 + just
$ git clone https://github.com/w0rxbend/ergodox && cd ergodox
$ just setup   # .venv + build123d
$ just model   # STEP, STL, fit-check DXF → exports/
$ just order   # PDF drawings + CNC order package
